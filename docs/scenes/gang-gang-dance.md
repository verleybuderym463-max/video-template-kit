# Gang Gang Dance: Blue-Lit Stage

## Short answer

AI Video Swap's Gang Gang Dance template puts your face on the lead dancer in a blue-lit stage performance. Prepare one clear human portrait. The hosted template supplies the choreography and scene, so you do not need to perform the dance yourself or upload a target video.

## Scene and inputs

On this site, **Gang Gang Dance** identifies the prepared stage-dance scene shown in the preview. One input photo maps to the lead dancer; it does not mean every person in the scene is replaced.

| Input | Type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo 1 | One human portrait | Central lead dancer |

Use a clear face photo with permission from its subject. Pet photos are outside this workflow. Other figures may remain in the scene.

## What the template supplies

The hosted workflow supplies the stage lighting, choreography, camera framing and prepared movement. The photo supplies the lead performer's identity. This is a prepared scene rather than a general editor for any video that shares its name.

## Prepare locally, generate online

Video Template Kit validates the declared input metadata and role count. It does not inspect the portrait's actual content, judge face visibility, check permission or animate a dancer. Review your photo yourself, then use the hosted page for cloud generation.

## What is not promised

This workflow does not replace every dancer or provide arbitrary choreography. The kit includes no video model, private source footage or rendering service. Valid metadata does not guarantee exact likeness, frame-by-frame motion or a successful render. No artist affiliation, soundtrack rights or official endorsement is claimed.

# Hit the Road Dude: Driver Meme

## Short answer

AI Video Swap's Hit the Road Dude template puts your face in the driver's role in the "this is private property" meme scene. Prepare one clear human portrait. The prepared scene retains its original vehicle and setting, so you do not need a car photo or a target video upload.

## Scene and inputs

This is a specific driving-meme workflow, rather than a tool for swapping cars or building an unrelated road scene.

| Input | Type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo 1 | One human portrait | Driver |

Use a portrait with a visible, unobstructed face and permission from its subject. A vehicle photo is not an image input for this template.

## What the template supplies

The hosted workflow supplies the original vehicle, scene, camera framing and prepared motion. Your portrait provides the driver's identity. You do not need to film yourself driving or find and upload the meme video.

## Prepare locally, generate online

Video Template Kit checks input metadata against the template's input count and roles. It cannot tell whether a file actually contains a good portrait, verify permission or generate the clip. After checking the metadata, inspect the photo yourself and use the hosted page for cloud generation.

## What is not promised

This template does not offer a vehicle selector or custom-car replacement. The kit does not contain a video model, private source footage or a rendering service. Passing metadata validation does not guarantee a particular likeness, performance or successful render. The scene name does not imply an official endorsement.

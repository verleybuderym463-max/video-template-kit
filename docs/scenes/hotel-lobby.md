# Hotel Lobby: Two-Person Microphone Performance

## Short answer

AI Video Swap's Hotel Lobby template places two people in a performance against an orange background beneath a hanging microphone. Prepare two separate human portraits: photo 1 maps to the left performer and photo 2 to the right. The hosted template supplies the scene and motion; a target video upload is not required.

## Scene and inputs

Here, **Hotel Lobby** is the name of a prepared two-person performance. It does not mean that you need to photograph a hotel lobby or provide a background image.

| Input | Type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo 1 | One human portrait | Performer on the left |
| Photo 2 | One human portrait | Performer on the right |

Use one person per file, with a visible face and even lighting. Confirm each person's permission to use their photo. Pet photos are outside this workflow.

## What the template supplies

The hosted workflow supplies the prepared performance scene, movement, camera framing and timing. The portraits provide the identities for the two mapped roles. You do not need to record a duet or upload a separate performance video.

## Prepare locally, generate online

Video Template Kit validates input metadata, including the selected template and expected input roles. It does not inspect photo pixels, judge lighting, check consent, replace faces or render a video. Review the actual portraits yourself, then use the hosted page if you want cloud generation.

## What is not promised

The kit does not include a video model, private source footage or a rendering service. Metadata validation is not a guarantee of likeness or a successful render. This scene is not an arbitrary hotel-background editor, and the name does not imply an official affiliation.

# Sega Dance Walk: Prepared Walking Dance

## Short answer

AI Video Swap's Sega Dance Walk template makes a walking-dance video using one human portrait. Your photo supplies the performer's identity, while the hosted template supplies the prepared walking choreography and scene. You do not need to record your own walk or upload a target video.

## Scene and inputs

The scene combines forward movement with a dance performance. **Sega Dance Walk** names this prepared workflow on AI Video Swap; it is not an instruction to choose an unrelated walking video.

| Input | Type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo 1 | One human portrait | Walking dancer |

Use a clear portrait with a visible face and permission from its subject. Pet photos are outside this workflow.

## What the template supplies

The hosted workflow supplies the walking choreography, setting, camera movement and timing. Your portrait provides the identity for the mapped performer. A separate motion-reference upload is not required.

## Prepare locally, generate online

Video Template Kit checks the declared template and input-role metadata. It cannot determine whether a photo is suitable, verify permission or turn the photo into a moving video. Inspect the actual image yourself, then use the hosted page for cloud generation.

## What is not promised

This is not a general motion-transfer editor for arbitrary choreography. The kit does not include a video model, private source footage or a rendering service. Valid metadata does not guarantee exact identity preservation, frame-by-frame reproduction or a successful render. The name does not establish a relationship with a game publisher or an official trend creator.

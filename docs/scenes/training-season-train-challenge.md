# Training Season Train Challenge: Phone-on-Train-Style Scene

## Short answer

AI Video Swap's Training Season Train Challenge template uses one full-body person photo for a prepared phone-on-train-style platform scene. The template supplies the station setting and motion reference. You do not need to film at a real train, attach your phone to one or upload a target video.

## Scene and inputs

Here, **Training Season Train Challenge** identifies a person-and-phone scene beside a train. It is a prepared video workflow, rather than a song-editing tutorial or a static meme generator.

| Input | Type | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo 1 | One full-body human photo | Person in the train-platform scene |

Choose a photo with the person's face, body and legs visible, and obtain permission to use it. A platform photo cannot substitute for the person photo: the template supplies the setting.

## What the template supplies

The hosted workflow supplies the train-platform setting and prepared movement. The photo provides the person's identity. Earlier frame inspection of the public preview showed a person holding a phone, approaching the train and turning back.

## Current verification boundary

The current preview has not been reviewed here through uninterrupted playback. Earlier sampled frames did not clearly establish a complete sequence in which a train carries the phone away and the person chases it. A complete chase is therefore **not a verified output claim in this documentation**. Inspect the preview and current hosted workflow before choosing this scene for a specific action sequence.

## Prepare locally, generate online

Video Template Kit checks declared input metadata, not photo pixels. It cannot confirm that a photo is full-body, evaluate image quality, check permission or generate video. Review the actual photo yourself, then use the hosted page for cloud generation.

## What is not promised

The kit contains no video model, private source footage or rendering service. Metadata validation does not guarantee a successful render, exact likeness or the full online trend's choreography. No particular audio track or artist relationship is claimed.
